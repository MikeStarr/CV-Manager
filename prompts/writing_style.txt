Are bullets concise, outcome‑focused, and consistently formatted?

2. TONE & POSITIONING
Does it sound like a senior delivery and platform transformation leader?
Is the language confident, specific, and grounded in real experience?
Does it avoid vague adjectives and job‑ad style wording?
Does it avoid empty claims and instead show lived detail?
3. CLARITY, CONTEXT & IMPACT
Are achievements clear and measurable?

General Writing Style Rules
Use simple, plain language and short sentences.
Avoid AI‑sounding phrases or hype.
Be direct and concise.
Keep the tone natural — write as people speak.
Avoid marketing language or exaggeration.
Keep it honest.
Casual grammar is fine if it sounds human.
Avoid filler adjectives.
Prioritise clarity.

Avoid generic CV boilerplate such as “demonstrated ability”, “proven track record”, “responsible for”, “worked on”, “successfully”, or any phrasing that sounds like a job advert or template
